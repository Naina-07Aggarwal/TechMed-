Our Specialities 

        At TechMed Hospital, we offer a wide range of specialized medical services to meet  the diverse needs of our patients. 
        Our team of expert physicians and healthcare professionals are dedicated to providing the highest quality care using 
        the latest advancements in medical technology.

Speciality List

Cardiology

Our Cardiology department provides comprehensive care for all types of heart conditions. Our team of Cardiologists offers the latest in diagnostic and treatment options, including advanced imaging, minimally invasive procedures, and cardiac rehabilitation.

Nuerology

The Nuerology department at TechMed Advanced Hospital specializes in diagnosing and treating disorders of the nervous system. Our Neurologists are experts in managing conditions such as epilepsy, multiple scelrosis, stroke and nueromuscular diseases.

Oncology

Our Oncology department is dedicated to providing comprehensive cancer care including Chemotherapy, Radiation Therapy and Surgical Oncology. Our multidisciplinary team works together to create personalized treatment plans for each patient.

Orthopedics

The Orthopedics department offers a wide range of services for the treatment of musculoskeletal conditions. Our orthopedic surgeons are skilled in joint replacement, sports medicine, spine surgery, and trauma care.

Pediatrics

Our Pediatrics department provides comprehensive care for infants, children, and adolescents. We offer preventive care, immunizations, and treatment for a variety of pediatric conditions to ensure the health and well-being of your child.
