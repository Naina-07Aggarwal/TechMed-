Visiting Hours

Introduction to Visiting Hours

 We understand the importance of visitors in the healing process. Our visiting hours are designed to 
 provide patients with the support they need from family while ensuring they get adequate rest and care.

General Visiting Hours

Our general visiting hours as follows:

  • Monday To Friday:- 10AM To 8PM
  • Saturday And Sunday:- 10AM To 6PM

Department-Specific Visiting Hours

Different departments have specific visiting hours and policies. Please see the details below:

  • Intensive Care Unit(ICU):- 12PM To 6PM
  • Neonatal Intensive Care(NICU):- 12PM To 4PM, Parents Allowed 24/7
  • Pediatrics:- 10AM to 8PM, Parents Allowed 24/7
  • Maternity/Women's Health:- 10AM To 8PM, Support Person Allowed 24/7
  • Surgery/Post-Operative Care:- 10AM To 8PM
  • Emergency Department:- Dependent On Patient Condition And Department Descretion

Visiting Policies

We have special visiting policies to ensure the comfort and safety of our patients.

  • Quiet Hours: 7AM To 8PM(Visits during these hours should be limited and quiet)
  • Age Restrictions: Visitors under the age of 12 are not allowed in ICU and NICU.
  • Number of Visitors: Maximum of 2 visitors per patient at any time.
