Cashless Treatment Facility

Our hospital offers a cashless treatment facility, allowing you to receive treatment without paying upfront. The insurance company settles all the bill directly with us. Follow these steps to avail the cashless treatment:

  1. Check with your insurance provider if cashless treatment is available at our hospital.
  2. Obtain a pre-authorization form from your insurance provider or our hospital's insurance helpdesk.
  3. Submit the completed form along with the necessary documents to our insurance helpdesk.
